# Acceptable Use Policy

**Organization:** {{org_name}}
**Effective date:** {{effective_date}}
**Policy owner:** {{security_officer}}

## Purpose

This policy explains what {{org_name}} expects of personnel when they use company devices, accounts, networks, and information. Its aim is to protect people, customers, and the company from harm caused by careless or inappropriate use, while allowing staff to work productively.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary workers, and covers:

- Company-issued laptops, phones, and other devices.
- Personally owned devices used to access company email, chat, or applications.
- Company accounts, networks, and SaaS applications.
- All information belonging to {{org_name}} or entrusted to it by customers and partners.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Security officer ({{security_officer}})** owns this policy, defines endpoint security standards, and investigates suspected violations.
- **IT** issues and configures devices, enforces the endpoint controls described here, and monitors compliance.
- **Managers** ensure their teams understand this policy and report concerns.
- **People Operations** administers the disciplinary process and records policy acknowledgements.
- **All personnel** must read, acknowledge, and follow this policy.

## Policy statements

### Code of conduct

1. Personnel must act honestly, ethically, and lawfully when using company resources, and must follow the {{org_name}} code of conduct.
2. Personnel must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Company systems must not be used to harass, threaten, discriminate against, or bully anyone.
3. Personnel must disclose conflicts of interest to their manager and must not use company information for personal gain.

### General use

4. Company resources are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted if it is lawful, does not interfere with work, does not consume significant resources, and does not violate any other part of this policy.
5. Personnel must access only the systems and data they are authorized to access for their role. Attempting to bypass access controls, probe systems, or access another person's account is prohibited unless explicitly authorized in writing as part of security testing.
6. Personnel must not install unlicensed software, pirated content, or software from untrusted sources on company devices.

### Prohibited activities

Personnel must not use company resources to:

- Create, store, or share illegal, sexually explicit, violent, or hateful material.
- Send unsolicited bulk messages or chain letters.
- Mine cryptocurrency or run personal commercial ventures.
- Disable, tamper with, or circumvent security tools.
- Transfer company or customer data to personal email, personal cloud storage, or unapproved applications.
- Use unapproved AI tools with company data (see the AI Acceptable Use Policy).

### Endpoint security

7. All company laptops and desktops must have full-disk encryption enabled. IT must verify encryption status through device management, and unencrypted devices must not access company systems.
8. Devices must lock automatically after no more than 15 minutes of inactivity and require authentication to unlock. Personnel must lock their screen manually whenever they leave a device unattended.
9. All company workstations must run the approved anti-malware or endpoint detection tool, kept up to date. Personnel must not disable it or create exclusions without IT approval.
10. Operating systems and applications must have security updates installed within 14 days of release, and critical updates as soon as practical.
11. Personally owned mobile devices used for company email or chat must have a passcode, encryption, and a supported operating system version, and must be enrolled in mobile management where required.

### Information handling

12. Information must be handled according to its classification. Confidential and restricted information must not be shared outside {{org_name}} without authorization and an appropriate agreement.
13. Personnel must not discuss confidential information in public places or leave printed documents unattended.
14. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours so they can be locked or wiped remotely.
15. Suspected security incidents, phishing messages, or policy violations must be reported to the security team immediately.

### Monitoring and privacy

16. {{org_name}} may monitor the use of company systems, devices, and networks for security, compliance, and operational purposes, in accordance with applicable law. Personnel should have no expectation of privacy in company systems beyond that provided by law.

### Return of assets

17. Personnel must return all company devices, access badges, and information on or before their last working day.

## Exceptions

Exceptions, such as a need for software not on the approved list, must be requested through IT and approved by the security officer. Approved exceptions must be documented and reviewed at least annually.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy will be handled through the {{org_name}} disciplinary process, which must be applied consistently and in proportion to the severity of the violation. Outcomes may range from a recorded warning to termination of employment or contract, and illegal activity may be reported to authorities. Disciplinary records must be retained by People Operations.

## Review

This policy must be reviewed at least annually by the security officer and People Operations. All personnel must acknowledge this policy when they join and after each material revision.
