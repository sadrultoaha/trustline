# Code of Conduct

**Effective date:** {{effective_date}}
**Policy owner:** {{security_officer}}

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct describes how people at {{org_name}} are expected to behave toward each other, customers, partners, and the public. It supports a culture of integrity, which is the foundation of the trust customers place in {{org_name}} and of an effective security program.

## Scope

This Code applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and board members of {{org_name}}, at work, at company events, when representing the company, and in online spaces connected to their work.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Leadership** must model the behaviors in this Code and ensure it is applied consistently.
- **People / HR team** administers the Code, receives reports, and coordinates investigations and disciplinary action.
- **Security Officer ({{security_officer}})** tracks acknowledgement of the Code alongside other policies and handles reports that involve information security.
- **Managers** must address conduct concerns raised with them and escalate serious matters to HR.
- **All personnel** must read, acknowledge, and follow this Code, and speak up when they see behavior that conflicts with it.

## Policy statements

### Acknowledgement

1. All personnel must acknowledge this Code within 30 days of joining and at least annually thereafter.
2. At least {{policy_acceptance_threshold}}% of active personnel must have a current acknowledgement of this Code.

### Respect and fair treatment

1. Personnel must treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism.
2. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, and retaliation on the basis of any characteristic protected by law, or for any other reason, are prohibited.
3. Employment decisions must be based on merit, qualifications, and business need.

### Integrity and honesty

1. Personnel must be honest in their communications, records, and reports, including timesheets, expenses, and audit evidence.
2. Falsifying records, misrepresenting the status of security controls, or concealing errors or incidents is prohibited.
3. Personnel must not offer, give, request, or accept bribes or improper payments. Gifts and hospitality must be modest, infrequent, and disclosed to a manager when above a nominal value set by HR.

### Conflicts of interest

1. Personnel must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of {{org_name}}.
2. Potential conflicts, such as outside employment with a competitor, financial interests in a supplier, or personal relationships affecting reporting lines, must be disclosed in writing to a manager or HR.

### Protecting information and assets

1. Personnel must protect confidential information belonging to {{org_name}}, its customers, and its partners, during and after their engagement.
2. Personnel must follow the information security policies, including acceptable use, data classification, and access control requirements.
3. Company assets must be used for legitimate business purposes and protected from loss, theft, and misuse.
4. Suspected security incidents, including lost devices or phishing, must be reported promptly through the designated channel.

### Compliance with laws

1. Personnel must comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including data protection, anti-corruption, competition, export control, and securities laws.
2. Personnel must not trade on, or share, material non-public information about {{org_name}} or other companies.

### Speaking up

1. Personnel are expected to report suspected violations of this Code, the law, or company policies to their manager, HR, {{security_officer}}, or through any anonymous reporting channel {{org_name}} provides.
2. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible and investigated promptly and fairly.
3. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited and will itself be treated as a violation of this Code.

### Disciplinary process

1. Alleged violations must be investigated by HR, with involvement from {{security_officer}} or legal counsel where relevant.
2. Individuals must be informed of the allegation and given an opportunity to respond before a decision is reached.
3. Sanctions must be proportionate and may include a warning, additional training, removal of access, suspension, or termination of employment or contract.
4. Investigation outcomes and sanctions must be documented in personnel records.

## Exceptions

No exceptions are permitted to the requirements of this Code relating to legal compliance, harassment, or retaliation. Other questions of interpretation must be referred to HR.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code are handled through the disciplinary process described above. Where conduct may be unlawful, {{org_name}} may refer the matter to the appropriate authorities.

## Review

The People team and {{security_officer}} review this Code at least annually. Material changes must be communicated to all personnel and re-acknowledged.
