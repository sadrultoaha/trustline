# Human Resources Security Policy

**Effective date:** {{effective_date}}
**Policy owner:** {{security_officer}}

## Purpose

People are central to {{org_name}}'s security. This policy ensures that personnel are suitable for their roles, understand their security responsibilities, and lose access promptly when they leave or change roles.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, interns, and contractors who have access to {{org_name}} systems or information, across the full lifecycle: recruitment, onboarding, employment, role changes, and departure.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Security Officer ({{security_officer}})** owns this policy, maintains the security awareness program, and monitors policy acknowledgement and training completion.
- **People / HR team** conducts background checks, manages contracts and personnel records, and initiates onboarding and offboarding.
- **Hiring managers** define role access needs, confirm onboarding tasks are completed, and notify HR of departures and role changes without delay.
- **IT / Operations** provisions and removes access in line with this policy.
- **All personnel** must complete required training, acknowledge policies, and protect company information.

## Policy statements

### Before employment

1. Background checks must be completed for all employees and for contractors with access to Confidential or Restricted data before they are granted such access, to the extent permitted by local law.
2. Checks must be proportionate to the role and should include identity verification, right to work, employment history, and, where lawful and relevant, criminal record checks.
3. Results must be reviewed by HR, and any concern must be escalated to {{security_officer}} before access is granted.
4. Job descriptions for roles with significant security responsibilities must describe those responsibilities.

### Terms and conditions

1. All personnel must sign a confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement before receiving access to company information. For employees this may be part of the employment contract.
2. Contracts must state that security obligations, including confidentiality, continue after employment ends.

### Onboarding

1. A documented onboarding checklist must be completed for every new hire, covering contract and NDA signature, account provisioning, equipment issuance, training, and policy acknowledgement.
2. Access must be granted according to the role and approved by the hiring manager, consistent with the Access Control Policy.
3. New personnel must complete security awareness training within 30 days of their start date.
4. New personnel must read and acknowledge the information security policies within 30 days of their start date.

### During employment

1. All personnel must complete security awareness training at least annually. Training must cover phishing, password and MFA practices, data handling, incident reporting, and relevant policy changes.
2. Personnel must re-acknowledge applicable policies at least annually and after material policy changes. At least {{policy_acceptance_threshold}}% of active personnel must have current acknowledgements at any time.
3. {{security_officer}} must track training completion and acknowledgement rates and follow up with personnel who are overdue.
4. Role changes must trigger a review of access. Access no longer required for the new role must be removed within five business days of the change.

### Personnel records

1. HR must maintain complete records for each person, including start date, role, manager, signed agreements, background check status, training completion, policy acknowledgements, and end date.
2. Personnel records must be kept in a system with access restricted to authorized HR staff and must be retained according to legal retention requirements.
3. The personnel list must be reconciled with the identity provider at least every {{access_review_days}} days to identify accounts without a matching active person.

### Termination and offboarding

1. Managers or HR must notify IT of a departure as soon as it is known, and no later than the last working day.
2. All access to company systems must be removed within one business day of the person's last working day. For involuntary terminations, access must be removed at or before the time the person is notified.
3. A documented offboarding checklist must be completed, covering account deactivation, transfer of data ownership, return of equipment and badges, and a reminder of continuing confidentiality obligations.
4. Shared credentials known to the departing person must be rotated.

### Disciplinary process

1. {{org_name}} must maintain a formal, fair disciplinary process for personnel who violate security policies.
2. Sanctions must be proportionate to the severity and intent of the violation and may range from additional training to termination of employment or contract.
3. Disciplinary actions must be documented in personnel records.

## Exceptions

Exceptions, such as delayed background checks where local processes are slow, must be approved by {{security_officer}} and HR, documented with compensating controls such as restricted access, and closed within 60 days.

## Enforcement

Failure to complete required training or acknowledgements may result in suspension of system access until completed. Other violations are handled under the disciplinary process above.

## Review

{{security_officer}} and the People team review this policy at least annually and after significant changes to employment law or organizational structure.
