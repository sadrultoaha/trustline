# Physical Security Policy

**Effective date:** {{effective_date}}
**Policy owner:** {{security_officer}}

## Purpose

Physical access to devices, paper records, and network equipment can bypass even strong technical controls. This policy sets the physical protections {{org_name}} applies to its premises, equipment, and media, and explains how responsibility is shared with cloud and colocation providers.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- All offices and workspaces leased or operated by {{org_name}}, including shared and co-working spaces.
- Equipment and media holding company information, wherever located, including home offices.
- All employees, contractors, and visitors.

Production infrastructure hosted by cloud providers is physically protected by those providers. Their controls are assessed under the Vendor Management Policy, for example through review of independent audit reports.

## Roles and responsibilities

- **Security Officer ({{security_officer}})** owns this policy, approves physical access controls, and reviews access logs and provider assurance reports.
- **Office or facilities manager** administers badges and keys, the visitor process, and office security measures.
- **All personnel** must protect company equipment and information in the office and when working remotely.

## Policy statements

### Office access

1. Office entrances must be secured with badge, key, or code-based access controls.
2. Access credentials must be issued only to personnel with a business need, recorded in an access register, and collected or deactivated on the person's last working day.
3. Lost badges or keys must be reported immediately and deactivated within one business day.
4. The access register must be reviewed every {{access_review_days}} days to remove access that is no longer required.
5. Shared or co-working spaces must provide lockable storage or a lockable room for any company equipment left on site.

### Visitors

1. Visitors must sign in, show identification where practical, and be escorted in non-public areas.
2. Visitor logs must record name, organization, host, and time in and out, and must be retained for at least 12 months.
3. Visitors must not be left unattended in areas containing network equipment or Confidential information.

### Secure areas

1. Network and server equipment kept on premises must be in a locked room or cabinet accessible only to authorized personnel.
2. Where installed, CCTV footage covering entrances and secure areas should be retained for at least 30 days, subject to local privacy law.

### Clear desk and clear screen

1. Confidential and Restricted paper documents must be locked away when not in use and at the end of each day.
2. Screens must be locked whenever a device is left unattended, and must lock automatically in line with the Asset Management Policy.
3. Whiteboards and printers must be cleared of Confidential information after use.

### Equipment security

1. Laptops must not be left unattended in public places or visible in vehicles.
2. Equipment taken off site remains subject to company policies, and loss or theft must be reported within 24 hours.
3. Personnel working remotely must use a private workspace where screens cannot be readily overlooked when handling Confidential information, and must not let household members use company devices.

### Secure disposal of media

1. Paper containing Internal, Confidential, or Restricted information must be cross-cut shredded or placed in locked confidential-waste bins.
2. Electronic media and devices must be sanitized or physically destroyed before disposal or reuse, as defined in the Asset Management Policy.
3. Disposal vendors must provide certificates of destruction, which must be retained for at least three years.

### Environmental protections

1. On-premises equipment rooms should have appropriate protection against power loss, overheating, fire, and water damage.

## Exceptions

Exceptions, such as a temporary event venue without access controls, must be approved in advance by {{security_officer}} with documented compensating measures.

## Enforcement

Personnel who violate this policy, including by sharing badges or allowing tailgating, may be subject to disciplinary action. Unauthorized visitors may be removed from the premises.

## Review

{{security_officer}} reviews this policy at least annually, when offices open, move, or close, and after any physical security incident.
