ACME CORP — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 3.2 · Approved by: Ada Lovelace (CEO) · Approval date: 2026-02-03 · Next review: 2027-02-03

1. Purpose
This information security policy establishes the security program of Acme Corp (the ISMS) and the
principles by which we protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer data.

2. Scope
All employees, contractors, systems and data processed by Acme Corp.

3. Roles and responsibilities
The CEO owns the security program. Grace Hopper (Staff Engineer) is the designated security officer.

4. Policy statements
- Access is granted on least privilege and reviewed quarterly.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for all workforce systems.
- Production data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Security incidents are handled according to the Incident Response Plan.

5. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually and re-approved by management.
